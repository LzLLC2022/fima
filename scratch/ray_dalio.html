Ray Dalio Portfolio

Bridgewater Associates Holdings

Ray Dalio, an American entrepreneur, and hedge fund manager who currently resides in Greenwich with his family. He studied at Long Island University (LIU) as well as Harvard Business. He founded Bridgewater Associates in 1975 which now has become one of the world's most reputable investment firms. He has deployed multiple strategies to generate success and has made many commonly used practices popular. His net worth is $20 billion.
Ray Dalio’s portfolio emphasizes broad U.S. equity market exposure plus leading technology and platform stocks—an approach built around diversified investment across major sectors and high-quality growth names.
1) SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust (SSgA Active Trust) — A core S&P 500 index investment that provides diversified exposure across large-cap U.S. stocks, aligning with a risk-aware, portfolio construction mindset.
2) iShares Core S&P 500 ETF (BlackRock Institutional Trust Company N.A.) — Another highly liquid, low-cost S&P 500 exposure investment, reinforcing Dalio’s preference for broad-market stocks and diversification within an investor-focused portfolio.
3) NVIDIA Corp — A technology and semiconductor leader positioned for AI and accelerated computing demand; a concentrated stock investment reflecting confidence in dominant market platforms and secular growth.
4) Broadcom Inc — A diversified infrastructure and semiconductors investment tied to networking, connectivity, and enterprise technology trends, suggesting a focus on durable cash flow and strategic exposure within the stocks portfolio.
5) Amazon.com Inc. — A major cloud, e-commerce, and logistics platform investment representing long-term growth potential, with Dalio’s portfolio benefiting from resilient, scale-driven market leadership.
Overall, Dalio’s investment stance highlighted by these top stocks and ETFs combines broad S&P 500 diversification with select high-impact technology and platform names—an investor approach aimed at balancing stability and growth potential within a modern portfolio of stocks.
